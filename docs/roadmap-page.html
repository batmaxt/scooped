Scooped · Plan of Record · September 2026

Road to Opening Day

Scooped is real-time flavor availability near you, wrapped in a social app people enjoy using. Every check-in, scan, and sighting is a data point wearing a fun costume. Any feature that produces neither delight in the first 30 seconds nor richer availability data gets cut. Everything below serves one date: opening day of ice cream season, April/May 2027.

Phase 2 · After densityThe shop partner loop

The business portal already exists (claim flow, dashboard, flavor board, admin approval). Phase 2 gives shops a reason to touch it daily — then makes it self-updating.

The killer pitch: "Connect your Square"

One-click OAuth and the shop's flavor board syncs itself from their point-of-sale forever. Nothing to remember, no daily button. Stronger than anything the incumbents offer — and strategically sweet, since POS platforms are natural acquirers of apps built on their rails. Toast and Clover follow.

  • Next
    The 10-second daily updateTwo buttons: 📸 "Scan today's board" (owner-trusted, auto-approved) and ✓ "Same as yesterday."
  • Next
    Update = marketing blastWhen a shop posts today's flavors, pushes fire to every user with a matching alert. The pitch: "Free customer notifications. You just tell us what's in the case."
  • Next
    Verified fresh todayBadge on the location page; demand stats on the dashboard ("212 people are waiting on flavors you carry").
  • Next
    Menu-as-signage bundlePrintable QR table-tent + web embed — the retention hook that keeps shops updating.
Free foreverVerification, menu upkeep, alerts, the daily update. Fresh data can never sit behind a shop's wallet — that pricing mistake killed a competitor and feeds the incumbent's #1 complaint.
ChargedAnalytics and demand insights, promotions, signage extras. Outputs, never inputs.
